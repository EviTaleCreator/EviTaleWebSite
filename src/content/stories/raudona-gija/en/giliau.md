## Behind the veil of the tale: the psychological layer

**Symbolism:** The hawthorn bush stands for the protective barriers, thorns and boundaries a child or adult builds when facing fear or insecurity. The red thread is a metaphor for secure attachment: an invisible but strong emotional bond with a close person that makes it possible to explore the world even when it feels frightening.

### Questions for a bedtime talk with a child or teenager

- What helps you feel safe when you end up somewhere unfamiliar or uncomfortable?
- Have you ever shut others out with your thorns, when you really just wanted a warm conversation?
