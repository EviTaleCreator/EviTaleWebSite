# Pienių pūkas: Gemini promptai

Kiekvienam paveikslėliui yra vienas pilnas promptas viename bloke. Nukopijuokite visą bloką ir įklijuokite į Gemini.

Kiekviena scena turi kitokį žiūrėjimo kampą, mažą istoriją kampe ir paslėptą boružėlę. Raudono siūlo šioje pasakoje nėra, nes jo nėra tekste.

Tvarka:
1. Pirmiausia Viršelis (prisegti notrelės viršelį kaip stiliaus pavyzdį). Kai patiks, jis bus stiliaus pavyzdys kitiems.
2. Tada veikėjų lapas: pienė ir Pūkė.
3. Tada scenos 01–12, tame pačiame Gemini pokalbyje.

Mažyliams: 01–08. Vaikams: 01, 09, 10, 04, 06, 08. Vyresniems: 11, 12.

Prisegti: notrelės viršelį (stiliaus pavyzdys).

```
Use the attached image as the STYLE REFERENCE: the same technique, palette and simplicity, but a completely new scene. Do not copy any plants, animals, objects or composition from it.

Create a children's picture book illustration, hand-made in opaque gouache with colored-pencil details on warm off-white paper. Visible pencil strokes and hatching, matte gouache with dry-brush edges, paper grain showing through. Simplified, slightly naive, characterful shapes. Even daylight, like a contemporary award-winning picture book.

PALETTE: graphite blue-grey #2d3142, sage green #8fa58c, poppy red #e2553e as the one bright accent, butter yellow #f3d58a, off-white paper #f7f5f0. Other colors muted. Lots of paper white.

VIEWPOINT: low, at ground level beside the fence post, looking up at the seed head against the sky.

SCENE: a May morning. At the corner of an old weathered wooden plank fence, silver-grey with yellow lichen spots and rusty nails, one board missing; a white dusty gravel road beside it; a low green meadow beyond; far away two rows of very old dark linden trees (an alley to a manor park), grows the mother dandelion: a common dandelion plant at the corner post of the fence, a flat rosette of deeply toothed lion-tooth leaves in sage green, hollow pale stems; no face, it shows feelings only by how it leans and bends. Its flower has turned into a perfectly round white seed head (a dandelion clock). On the very top sits Pūkė: one dandelion seed a little bigger than the others, a feathery white parachute on a thin stalk with a small brown seed below; no face, it shows feelings only by tilt and movement, leaning forward and looking towards the far linden trees. Around the fence the grass is full of yellow dandelion flowers.

STORY DETAILS TO DISCOVER: a cat's tail disappears through the gap of the missing board; a small red ladybug climbs the fence post.

COMPOSITION: the white seed head large in the center against calm empty paper sky; the far linden alley small on the horizon.

FORMAT: horizontal, 4:3. Keep the main subjects in the central area with space on all sides (the picture will be cropped differently on the website).

AVOID: glow, sparkles, light rays, mist, vignette, dark background, digital fantasy painting, airbrush, 3D, photorealism, faces on plants, text, signatures, frames.
```
